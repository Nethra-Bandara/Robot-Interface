# Species Identification Knowledge Base

## Sri Lankan Leopard (Panthera pardus kotiya)
- **Identification**: Large cat with a tawny/rusty-yellow coat and dark spots/rosettes. Smaller rosettes than African leopards.
- **Habitat**: Found in all habitats in Sri Lanka, including scrublands, rainforests, and montane forests.
- **Behavior**: Solitary and nocturnal, but also active during dawn and dusk.
- **Status**: Endangered.

## Sri Lankan Blue Magpie (Urocissa ornata)
- **Identification**: Bright blue body with a chestnut-red head and wings. Red bill and legs. Long tail with white tips.
- **Habitat**: Endemic to the wet zone rainforests of Sri Lanka.
- **Behavior**: Found in small groups, very vocal.

## Red-faced Malkoha (Phaenicophaeus pyrrhocephalus)
- **Identification**: Large bird with dark green back, white-tipped tail, and a prominent red patch around the eyes.
- **Habitat**: Endemic to Sri Lanka, found in dense forests.

## Sloth Bear (Melursus ursinus inornatus)
- **Identification**: Shaggy black coat, long muzzle, and a V-shaped white mark on the chest.
- **Habitat**: Dry zone forests.
